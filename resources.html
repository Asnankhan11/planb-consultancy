Career resources you can use before your next application

Read the guides, complete the checklists and prepare at your own pace. Start with the task in front of you: a resume to send, an interview to attend or an offer to check.

By Plan B Careers. Published 8 November 2026.

Choose the guide for your next step

Build a resume that fits the vacancy

Choose relevant skills and projects, explain your experience honestly and download an editable Word template. Includes a final check before sending.

Prepare for a fresher interview

Use a 60-minute practice plan, prepare truthful examples and choose questions about the role, shifts and next steps.

Understand salary and compare offers

Separate CTC, gross and take-home pay. Compare fixed pay, incentives, travel costs and the terms that should appear in writing.

Check a work-from-home job

Verify the recruiter, work location, equipment and fee requests. Learn what to do if you suspect financial cyber fraud.

Download the editable resume template

Use the existing Plan B Careers Word template as a starting point. Replace every bracketed prompt, remove unused sections and tailor it to the vacancy. Read the resume guide before sending your application.

About these resources

These guides are published by Plan B Careers for candidates preparing applications and making career decisions. Examples are labelled when they are illustrative. Employer conditions, admission requirements and official deadlines should be checked at their original source.

You can read these pages and download the resume template without registering. For individual assistance, contact the Akola team and confirm the service scope and any charges for your circumstances.

If you find an outdated link or unclear advice, send the page address and correction so the team can check it.
