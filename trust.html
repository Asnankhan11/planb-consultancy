Trust starts with clear, checkable information

Understand what a listing says, what it does not establish and what to confirm before making a decision.

A listing is not an offer

Our published roles contain information provided for candidate enquiries. An open status does not guarantee that the employer is still hiring when you apply. Read the listed update date and confirm availability, location, pay basis and selection steps.

Employer relationships

Do not infer a formal partnership or authorization from an employer name in a listing. Ask which organization is hiring and whether Plan B is an authorized recruiter, an application-support intermediary or sharing a public opportunity.

What to verify

  1. Confirm the employer's legal identity and official contact channel.
  2. Ask for a written job description, actual workplace and equipment requirements.
  3. Separate guaranteed pay from incentives, commission, deductions and CTC.
  4. Discuss service charges and refund terms directly before agreeing or paying.
  5. Confirm an offer directly with the employer before resigning, relocating or making a financial commitment.

Reviews and outcome statistics

We do not use simulated reviews, fabricated activity feeds or an automatic trust percentage. Our business owner reports 6 years in business and more than 1,000 candidates placed, confirmed on 2026-11-08. These figures are shared by the business and are not an independently audited result. Ask the team for relevant evidence and permission-cleared references.

Private candidate verification

We do not publish candidate records through a public name, phone or email search. Contact the team to discuss an offer or application reference; do not share personal information publicly.
