# Plan B Careers: Akola and Vidarbha competitor research

Research date: **8 November 2026**. Site: **https://planbcareers.in/**.

This is a bounded search and page-content review supporting the SEO/AEO/GEO implementation. It is not a census of every competitor, a controlled Google ranking report, an assessment of service quality, or a measurement of AI recommendation share. The owner's report of approximately tenth position is an unverified starting observation.

## What the evidence suggests

1. **Job discovery and consultancy selection are different search markets.** Directory and job-board pages appeared frequently in the sampled Akola job queries. Employer-facing agencies and career advisers appeared for different intents. A single generic consultancy homepage cannot answer all of those tasks well.
2. **The useful competitive standard is specific information.** Sampled recruitment pages explain hiring stages; admissions pages explain the decision and application process; job directories expose location and category navigation. Plan B should make actual vacancy status, work location, eligibility, pay basis and application steps easier to inspect.
3. **Candidate guidance was a fixable first-party gap.** The original resources page contained three placeholder download links. Complete resume, interview, offer-comparison and remote-work safety guides now give candidates something useful before they contact the business.
4. **Regional relevance must be earned through useful decisions.** The Akola page now explains preparing for a local career discussion and checking study options. The Vidarbha page compares commuting, relocation and remote eligibility. These pages are distinct guides, not copied city-name variants.
5. **A search-visible page is not proof of a physical local office, reliable outcomes or authority.** Competitor claims remain self-published claims. Plan B's confirmed office is near City Kotwali, Akola; no additional offices, placement totals, partner counts or guaranteed results were invented.

## Method and evidence

Eight Firecrawl web searches were requested with location hint “Akola, Maharashtra, India”, returning 44 result entries before deduplication. Four English searches returned six entries each; the other four searches returned five each. The location hint does not establish a controlled Google device, geolocation or local-pack environment. Provider result position must not be presented as a measured Google rank.

Six relevant pages were then fetched fresh from the organisations' own sites with Firecrawl (maxAge: 0, main-content Markdown). All six reported HTTP 200. Search excerpts are labelled as such below; they are weaker evidence than inspected page content. We did not complete calls, submit forms, inspect private dashboards, or verify competitors' claimed results.

Raw search and page captures are stored under [evidence/competitors-2026-11-08/](evidence/competitors-2026-11-08/). They preserve the provider IDs and URLs for review. They are research records, not public website copy. Search feedback was submitted for all eight search calls.

### Search-quality cautions

- Some results for “career counselling” were vacancies for counsellors rather than services for students. They should not be counted as direct adviser competitors.
- A JobHai result used “Amravati, Nagpur” in its URL/title. That can denote a locality context rather than Amravati city; the sample does not establish coverage of the intended city.
- Marathi regional queries surfaced older social posts. A visible social result is not evidence that its vacancy is still open.
- Job-board and directory snippets can contain stale or inconsistent counts. None of those counts has been used as a Plan B benchmark.
- Repetition of city names on a page does not establish quality, relevance, a local office or recommendation by a search engine.

## What remains unmeasured and how to evaluate progress

**Not measured here:** actual Google organic/local-pack position; Google Business Profile access, completeness and reviews; Search Console/Bing indexing or impressions; verified backlinks; search volumes; competitor conversion rates; live Google AI Overview, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Copilot recommendations; owner-confirmed case outcomes; real-user Core Web Vitals.

After deployment and indexing, use a stable prompt/query set that separates:

- Akola placement consultancy and candidate-assistance intent.
- Akola fresher and work-from-home vacancy intent.
- Career guidance after school/graduation in Akola.
- Vidarbha/Nagpur/Amravati work-location decisions.
- Brand trust, service scope and fees.

For organic/local checks, record the exact query, date/time, search engine, device, location and whether the observed result is organic, local-pack or paid. Keep screenshots or an authorised rank export. For AI answers, record the engine/version, prompt, whether web search was used, full answer, cited URLs, brand mentions and recommendation context. Repeat the same prompts to expose variation; one answer is not a stable rank.

Use Search Console page/query impressions, clicks, indexing state and qualified enquiries as separate outcomes. Compare before and after periods, noting seasonality and vacancy changes. On-site changes can improve clarity and eligibility; this research cannot predict or guarantee a first-place ranking.

Further authority work should use owner-verified business details, real case records and consented feedback. No directory submissions, external profile edits, review requests, outreach, paid accounts or fabricated endorsements were performed in this workstream.
