How to use these listings

Distinguish the work involved

Data entry, barcode generation and inventory updating are different tasks. Check the actual duties, accuracy expectations, software and reporting process before choosing a role.

Check task-based payment carefully

When earnings depend on completed units, ask what counts as accepted work, how quality is checked and when payment is due. The maximum advertised figure is not a fixed monthly salary.

Keep your application relevant

List spreadsheet, typing, record-keeping and device skills you can demonstrate. Do not claim a typing speed or software qualification you have not checked.
