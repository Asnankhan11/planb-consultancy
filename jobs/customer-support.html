Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding eligibility, interview rounds, and selection criteria.

What is the difference between voice and non-voice customer support?

Voice customer support involves answering phone calls from customers to address queries or resolve complaints. Non-voice support is conducted via live chat, email, or ticketing systems without making or receiving voice calls.

Can freshers apply for international customer support positions?

Yes. Freshers with good English pronunciation and communication skills are eligible for international voice and chat processes. Training is provided by our hiring partners.
