How to use these listings

Check the setup before applying

Some roles need a laptop, stable broadband, a headset or power backup. A mobile-friendly listing is not evidence that every remote job can be done on a phone. Read the individual requirements.

Compare pay on the same basis

Fixed monthly pay, in-hand pay, CTC, commission and task-based earnings mean different things. An “up to” amount is not a promised salary. Ask about payout timing, deductions and targets.

Confirm where remote work is allowed

Remote describes the work arrangement. It does not mean applicants in every country or state are eligible. Ask the hiring team about location restrictions, training attendance and equipment checks.
