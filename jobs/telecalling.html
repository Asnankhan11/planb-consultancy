What is the salary structure for telesales and telecalling roles?

Entry-level telecallers earn between ₹15,000 and ₹22,000 fixed per month. High-performing executives earn additional performance incentives of ₹10,000 to ₹40,000 monthly.

Is English fluency mandatory for telecalling jobs?

No. While some corporate accounts require bilingual fluency (English and Hindi), multiple domestic campaigns specifically seek fluent Hindi, Marathi, or regional language speakers.
