Who Is Eligible?

Fast track pathways are evaluated and verified for various candidate profiles

Students with academic gaps seeking to resume their education.

Dropout students wanting to continue and complete their degrees.

Working professionals without completed degrees looking to upskill.

Candidates with prior academic credits eligible for credit transfer.

Please Note: Each case is individually evaluated based on specific university eligibility criteria. Outcomes depend on the candidate's academic history, chosen program, and institutional policies.

Compliance & Transparency

Our absolute commitment to ethical and lawful academic guidance

Plan B Careers does not issue degrees, certificates, or academic credentials directly. All admissions and academic pathways are processed strictly through recognized institutions and are subject to university policies, eligibility criteria, and regulatory compliance.

We do not promote, guide, or support any unauthorized, fake, or illegal academic documentation.

Students are encouraged to independently verify university recognition and registration details before enrollment.
