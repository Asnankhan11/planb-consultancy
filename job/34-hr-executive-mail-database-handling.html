What the work involves

Responsibilities and requirements reproduced from the source listing.

4 A.M Tea is hiring an HR Executive in Mumbai for email handling, candidate communication, and HR database operations.

Role & Compensation Overview:

  • Number of Posts: 1 Opening
  • Work Location: Mumbai, Maharashtra (Work From Office)
  • Relocation Policy: Only Mumbai-based candidates or candidates willing to relocate to Mumbai should apply
  • Qualification: Any Degree
  • Salary: ₹25,000 / month

Skills Required:

  • Fluent in English Communication (verbal and written)
  • Mail Handling and official email correspondence
  • Database Handling and maintaining accurate candidate/employee records

Key Responsibilities:

  • Manage official HR email communications, candidate enquiries, and follow-ups
  • Maintain, verify, and update structured HR databases and recruitment trackers
  • Coordinate with applicants and internal teams cleanly and professionally
