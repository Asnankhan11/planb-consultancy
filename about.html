Our Mission & Foundation

Bridging Bharat's Ambition With India's Real Job Market.

Founder's Note

"Everyone deserves a solid backup plan that actually delivers."

When youth in non-metro cities complete their graduation, they face two brutal challenges: predatory agents demanding upfront fees for fake jobs, or lack of guidance on cracking real BPO, telecalling, and remote Work From Home opportunities.

At Plan B Careers, we do not operate on hollow promises. We partner directly with verified companies, train candidates on interview confidence, assist them with genuine college admissions, and maintain an open verification database so candidates and their parents always know they are safe.
