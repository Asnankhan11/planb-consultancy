Career support rooted in Akola

Plan B Careers, founded by Asnan Khan, supports job seekers and students with career conversations, application preparation and admission enquiries.

Practical help with the next decision

Our office is near City Kotwali, Akola, Maharashtra. We assist candidates from Akola and the wider Vidarbha region, including people considering remote opportunities. We do not claim a staffed branch in every city we serve.

We help you understand role requirements, prepare a resume and questions, and communicate an enquiry. The employer or institution controls selection, offers, terms and admission.

What to expect from us

  • A discussion about your qualification, experience and circumstances.
  • Clear separation between a published vacancy, a service enquiry and a confirmed offer.
  • A direct discussion of any service charges before you decide to proceed.
  • Encouragement to verify employer and institution information independently.

Read our listing and candidate-safety policy or arrange a conversation.
