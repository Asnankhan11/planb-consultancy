Discuss the service before you decide

We do not publish fixed service prices. The team discusses your circumstances, the work requested and any applicable charges directly.

Get the complete picture in writing

Service scope and any charges are discussed directly with the team for your situation. Ask for the scope, amount and applicable refund terms in writing before agreeing or paying. Hiring decisions remain with the employer.

  • Which service is being offered and what will actually be delivered?
  • What is the total amount, including any applicable taxes or third-party costs?
  • When would payment be due, and to which confirmed business recipient?
  • What happens if you cancel or the agreed service cannot be delivered?
  • What is controlled by Plan B and what is decided by the employer or institution?
