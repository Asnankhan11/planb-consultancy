Questions candidates ask

Where is Plan B Careers in Akola?
Our office is near City Kotwali, Akola, Maharashtra. Contact the Akola team to arrange a visit and confirm directions. We also assist candidates from across Vidarbha; a service area does not mean a separate office.
What are the charges?
Service scope and any charges are discussed directly with the team for your situation. Ask for the scope, amount and applicable refund terms in writing before agreeing or paying. Hiring decisions remain with the employer. Read how to discuss service fees.
Does Plan B Careers guarantee a job?
No. We provide career and application support. Employers decide interviews, hiring and compensation. Check a role's requirements and obtain written offer terms directly from the employer.
Can freshers apply for work-from-home jobs?
Only where the employer accepts freshers and you meet the equipment, language and location requirements. See listings marked suitable for freshers and read the full role details.
Can I get admission guidance in Akola?
You can discuss course choices, eligibility and document preparation with the team. Admission decisions, recognition and schedules must be checked with the relevant institution and official admission authority.
