A practical resume guide for freshers

Your resume should help an employer see what you can do and how it fits the vacancy. Start with accurate information, show evidence and tailor the file to each role.

By Plan B Careers. Published 8 November 2026.

What should a fresher put on a resume?

Include your name and contact details, education, skills relevant to the vacancy, and evidence from projects, internships, volunteering or work. A concise one-page resume is a useful starting point for a fresher. Add another page only when it contains relevant information.

  • Contact details: your name, city and state, a working phone number and professional email. Add a portfolio or LinkedIn link if it supports the application.
  • Short introduction: two or three specific lines about your qualification, relevant ability and target role.
  • Education: qualification, institution, completion year or expected completion date. Use accurate names and results.
  • Skills: tools, languages and tasks you can demonstrate. Describe your level honestly.
  • Evidence: projects, internships, employment or volunteering. Explain your contribution rather than listing a team’s achievements as your own.
  • Availability: mention location, joining availability or shift restrictions when the vacancy asks for them.

How do I tailor a resume to a job description?

  1. Read the role carefully. Mark the responsibilities, required qualification, work location, language and tools.
  2. Match real evidence. Next to each requirement, write one example from your experience. Leave a genuine skill gap visible rather than claiming expertise.
  3. Choose relevant content. Put the strongest matching project or experience first. Remove material that crowds out useful information.
  4. Use the employer’s terms accurately. If the vacancy says “Microsoft Excel” and you use Excel, name it. Repeating keywords without evidence makes the resume harder to read.
  5. Check the requested file type. Follow the application instructions. Before sending a PDF, confirm that the text can be selected and copied in reading order.

Show a skill with an example

Illustrative rewrites; use only facts that are true for you
Vague wordingA more useful description
“Good communication skills”“Explained registration steps to visitors during a college event and recorded their questions for the organiser.”
“Expert in Excel”“Used Excel to enter records, sort rows and check missing values for a college project.”
“Hardworking fresher”“Completed a group assignment on schedule and prepared the final presentation.”

These are examples of structure, not suggested achievements. Add a count, percentage or result only if you can explain how it was measured. A clear task without a statistic is better than an invented result.

Keep the file easy to read

Use familiar section headings and a simple reading order. Avoid placing essential contact information only inside an image. Check spelling, spacing, dates and links on both a phone and a larger screen. Name the file so the recruiter can identify you, for example FirstName-LastName-Customer-Support-Resume.pdf.

Do not attach Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, signatures or identity documents to a public resume. Share any necessary verification documents later through a confirmed employer process. Read how to check recruiter requests before sending sensitive information.

How do I explain a career break or no experience?

State dates accurately and focus on your current ability to do the role. A short explanation such as family responsibilities, study or personal leave can be enough. Include relevant learning or projects only when you actually completed them. You do not need to disclose private family or health details in a resume.

Final check before you apply

  • The phone number and email work, and every link opens.
  • The target role and important qualifications are easy to find.
  • Every date, skill and achievement is accurate.
  • Example text and bracketed template prompts have been removed.
  • The file opens correctly and follows the employer’s upload instructions.
  • The application is for a current listing with a clear location and hiring process.

Save a copy of the vacancy and the resume you sent. That makes interview preparation easier and helps you track which version each employer received.
