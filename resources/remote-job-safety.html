Check a work-from-home job before you apply

Verify the employer, the work and the terms before sharing documents or paying. This guide helps job seekers in Akola and across Vidarbha assess remote opportunities with clearer information.

By Plan B Careers. Published 8 November 2026.

How can I check whether a work-from-home job is genuine?

Confirm the employer independently, ask for a written job description, and check the pay, work location and selection process. Contact the organisation through a number or address you found independently. A company logo, a WhatsApp message or a video interview alone does not establish that a recruiter is authorised.

  1. Identify the employer. Ask for the legal company name, website, role title and recruiter’s official contact. Check that the details agree across the vacancy and the employer’s own channels.
  2. Confirm the vacancy. Use the employer’s published contact or careers page to check the opportunity. Do not rely only on the contact details sent in the message you are checking.
  3. Understand the work. Request responsibilities, employment type, eligible work location, hours, equipment and reporting arrangements.
  4. Clarify pay. Ask which amount is fixed, which is target-based, and when payment occurs. Use the offer comparison checklist.
  5. Check any money request. Ask who is collecting it, the service provided, written terms and receipt. Stop if you are being rushed to pay for a guaranteed selection.
  6. Protect sensitive information. Never share an OTP, UPI PIN, banking password or remote access to your device with a recruiter.

Requests that deserve an immediate pause

  • A recruiter promises guaranteed high earnings for simple tasks but will not explain the employer or actual work.
  • You must deposit money, recharge an account or complete paid tasks to unlock wages or withdraw a balance.
  • You are asked to scan a QR code, enter a UPI PIN or share an OTP to “receive salary”.
  • You are told to install an unfamiliar APK or screen-sharing application for verification.
  • The sender pressures you to pay immediately and refuses written terms or independent verification.
  • The documents, email domain, company name or payment recipient do not match, and the recruiter cannot explain why.

Pause and verify before continuing. One unusual detail does not establish fraud by itself; several inconsistencies or a request for security credentials are strong reasons to stop the interaction.

Does “remote” mean I can work from Akola?

Only if the employer allows your location. Remote roles can still have location restrictions, office training, fixed shifts or equipment requirements. Confirm that you can work from Akola or your town in Vidarbha, and ask whether any interview, onboarding or regular attendance is required elsewhere.

Is a fee proof that a recruiter is fraudulent?

A fee alone does not establish fraud. You need to know what specific service is being sold, who is charging it and what written terms apply. Keep a paid career service separate from an employer’s hiring decision. Do not treat a fee, registration or course purchase as a guarantee of a job.

For any Plan B Careers service, ask the team for the current scope and charges in writing before payment. Read the refund policy and keep the receipt. Do not assume every service or employer opportunity has the same terms.

What should I do if I suspect financial cyber fraud?

Stop further payments, preserve the messages, payment references and account details, and contact your bank or payment provider promptly through its official channel. For financial cyber fraud in India, call 1930 and report the incident through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. Follow the instructions given and retain your acknowledgement.

Official source: the Ministry of Home Affairs information published by PIB confirms the reporting portal and helpline. Reporting does not guarantee recovery of money.

Use official job-search resources as an additional check
