Privacy policy

How Plan B Careers collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 2026-11-11  |  Applies to planbcareers.in and related Plan B Careers services.

Plan B Careers ("we", "us", "our") is a career-support and recruitment consultancy based in Akola, Maharashtra, India. This privacy policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights under applicable Indian law — including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDPA) and the Information Technology Act 2000.

1. Data we collect and why

We collect only the information needed to provide the service you requested:

  • Name, mobile number, WhatsApp number — to contact you about your enquiry or application.
  • Email address — if provided, to send confirmation or follow-up messages.
  • City and location details — to match you to roles or institutions in your area.
  • Qualification and experience information — to assess eligibility for listed roles or admission programmes.
  • Resume / CV (file upload) — to share with the relevant recruiting employer or institution for the role you applied to. Upload only your own resume; do not include identity documents, bank details or OTPs.
  • Contact-form and WhatsApp messages — to handle your enquiry, using those platforms' own infrastructure.

We collect this data with your consent, given when you submit a form or initiate contact. Submission is voluntary; not providing information may mean we cannot process your request.

2. How we use your information

  • Respond to career, job or admission enquiries you have sent us.
  • Forward your resume and contact details to the specific employer or institution for the role you applied to.
  • Confirm receipt and progress of your application.
  • Comply with legal obligations applicable to us.

We do not use your information for unrelated marketing without your separate consent. We do not sell personal data.

3. Who we share your information with

Your application details (name, contact, resume) are shared with the recruiting employer or institution named in the job or admission listing you applied to. We ask you to confirm the intended recipient before sending sensitive documents.

Enquiries sent via WhatsApp or email are processed by those platforms under their own privacy policies. Our database and document-storage infrastructure is provided by third-party cloud services operating under their own data-processing terms.

We do not share personal data with any other third parties except where required by law or with your explicit instruction.

4. Cookies and website measurement

On public information pages (such as job listings, resources and the homepage), we load Google Analytics to measure page visits and contact-link clicks. We configure this measurement to exclude form field contents and resume links, and to strip URL query strings from recorded page paths.

We do not load Google Analytics on application forms, admission forms, the contact-message page, or the saved-jobs page.

Google Analytics stores a cookie on your browser. By continuing to browse our public pages, you accept this measurement. You may opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Google operates this service under Google's privacy policy.

5. Your browser's saved list

The saved-jobs feature stores selected job IDs locally on your device using browser localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to our servers. It is not an employer record. You can clear it at any time from the saved-jobs page.

6. Data retention

Application submissions are retained for the period necessary to process your application and fulfil related compliance requirements, typically not exceeding 12 months from submission unless a longer period is required by applicable law. Contact the team to request earlier deletion.

7. Data security

Access to candidate records is restricted to authorised personnel. Do not share OTPs, banking passwords or identity documents through our forms. If you believe your information has been compromised, contact us immediately.

8. Your rights under DPDPA 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable Indian laws, you have the right to:

  • Access — confirm whether we hold your personal data and obtain a summary.
  • Correction — request that inaccurate or incomplete data is corrected.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data where we no longer have a lawful basis to retain it.
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw the consent you gave when submitting your information. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.
  • Nominate — nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer (see section 10) with your name, the reference number if you have one, and a description of your request.

9. Children

Our services are intended for individuals who are at least 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from persons under 18 without verifiable parental or guardian consent. If you believe a minor has submitted information to us, contact us immediately.
