Privacy and candidate information

Understand the service and ask for clarification before deciding.

Updated 2026-11-08

Plan B Careers uses information you choose to submit—such as your name, contact details, city, qualifications and resume—to respond to career or admission enquiries and handle the requested application support.

Where information is used

Application handling may use our configured database, email and document-storage providers. Enquiries sent through WhatsApp or email also use those providers' systems and policies. Information relevant to an application may be shared with the recruiting team or employer for that request. Ask the team about the intended recipient before sending sensitive documents.

Website measurement

On public information pages, Google Analytics measures page use and contact-link clicks. We do not load Google Analytics on application, admission, contact-message or saved-data pages. Our manual events exclude form contents and resume links, and our page settings remove URL query strings. Google operates this measurement service under its own policies.

Your browser's saved list

The saved-jobs page keeps selected job IDs and submission references on your device. It is not an employer record, and you can clear it on that page. We do not use local browser storage as a verified candidate profile.

Use care with personal information

Do not upload banking credentials, OTPs or identity documents in the resume field. Candidate records are not provided through a public search. Administrative access is restricted.

Questions and requests

Contact contact.planbcareers@gmail.com for questions about your information, correction or deletion requests, or to discuss how a particular application is handled. Include a reference if you have one; do not send unnecessary identity documents in the initial message.
